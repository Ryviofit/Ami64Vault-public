# Ami64Vault

Ami64Vault is a local-first Commodore 64 and Amiga library and emulator
frontend for iPhone and Android.

The app does not include commercial games, proprietary C64 firmware or Amiga
Kickstart ROMs. Users provide files they are legally entitled to use.
